Circular No: SCE/ACAD/2026/41            Date: 01 September 2026

Sub: Semester 4 internal assessment schedule, arrear registration and library timings.

1. INTERNAL ASSESSMENT
   Internal Test II for all Semester 4 courses will be held from 17 September 2026 to
   20 September 2026. Portions cover units 1 to 4. Each test carries 20 marks and is
   scaled to 30 as per the academic regulations. Assignments and quizzes carry the
   remaining 20 marks of the 50-mark internal component.

2. ATTENDANCE
   A minimum of 75% attendance is required to appear for the end-semester examination.
   Students between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a medical certificate,
   countersigned by the mentor, within seven days of the last instruction day.
   Condonation is not automatic; the committee may refuse it.

3. ARREAR REGISTRATION
   Arrear registration for the November 2026 examination closes on 10 October 2026.
   An arrear candidate re-appears for the end-semester examination only; internal marks
   are retained. A student with more than four standing arrears at the end of Semester 4
   may not register for higher-semester courses until the count is brought down.

4. LIBRARY
   The central library works 09:00 to 17:00 on working days and 09:00 to 13:00 on the
   second Saturday. Reference section access requires the college ID card. Borrowed
   books are due in 14 days; the fine thereafter is Rs. 2 per day per book.

5. FEE
   The Semester 5 tuition fee of Rs. 42,500 is payable before 30 October 2026. Late
   payment attracts a fine of Rs. 500 per week, up to a maximum of Rs. 2,000.
